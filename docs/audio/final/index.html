Produced 2026-10-02 with ElevenLabs inside a 20,000 credit budget; loudness is baked into every file. Nobody has listened to these yet: every file passed objective checks (decode, duration, clipping, silence, loudness, seams) and nothing more. Start with the reward chime, the tend tock, the two beds families and three narration lines; the full numbers are in orchestration/runs/audio/REPORT.md.
