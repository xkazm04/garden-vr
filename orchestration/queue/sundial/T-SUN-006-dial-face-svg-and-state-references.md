## Goal
Two things the owner needs at R2: candidate frames for the anime-pushed look the references do not yet show (A/2-05-F1
the drawn dial in a real kitchen; A/2-05-F2 day 7), and a dial face that closes the round-3 gaps (more pencil
construction, looser line weight, richer washes with wet edges and granulation, a soil spill instead of flat paint, a soft
painted gnomon shadow), generated by script so it can be rebuilt. Bounded: max 3 compare rounds, max 24 generations.

## Steps
0. `git merge --no-edit main`. Create `orchestration/runs/sundial/T-SUN-006/`.
1. **Reference candidates**: `image_gen` 4 x A/2-05-F1 and 4 x A/2-05-F2 with the exact prompts and negatives (16:9) into
   `apps/sundial/Art/Source/ref-candidates/` with `.prompt.txt` sidecars and a `CANDIDATES.md` contact sheet. They are
   not anchors until the owner picks at R2.
2. **Face pipeline**: copy `dial_svg.mjs` to `apps/sundial/Art/Scripts/dial_face.mjs` and adapt: output to
   `apps/sundial/Assets/Art/Textures/dial_face.png` (2048); palette graded to the bible's measured column; more pencil
   construction (radial guides, compass arcs), line weight varying along strokes, wet-edge pooling and granulation on the
   washes, a stippled soil spill shape inside the bed, no text. Rasterise with system Chrome
   (`C:/Program Files/Google/Chrome/Application/chrome.exe`) through `playwright-core`; pin it in
   `apps/sundial/Art/Scripts/package.json` and say so in the report (a new tool dependency).
3. **Gnomon shadow decal**: a soft wash shadow (SVG, blurred edge, slight granulation) rotated by `gnomonDeg`; drop the
   realtime shadow casters if the decal reads better (report draws before/after).
4. **Compare loop** (max 3): capture G1 and G2, side-by-sides and same-pixel crops (washes, soil, gnomon shadow, tiles);
   findings as located corrections; keep each round; pick the best by the bible rubric, region by region (minimum counts).
5. Update `PROVENANCE.md` (face: painted by code, script path; candidates: generated).
6. Commit per sub-step.
